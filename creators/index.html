Creator Mode for Windows

AIVMeta Creator Mode is a Windows desktop tool that lets creators connect their own TikTok account and share their own local video content using TikTok's supported Content Posting flows.

Windows 0.1.0 beta — Production OAuth connection enabled for review/verification. Use Connect my TikTok account to sign in and authorize on TikTok through the live HTTPS broker, then return to review your account and local media. Uploads and posts remain disabled during validation. TikTok permissions, account settings and review status still apply; this is not a claim of TikTok Production approval.

Download Windows x64 beta

Unsigned portable ZIP. Extract all files and open Launch AIVMeta Creator.cmd. No separate Python or FFmpeg installation and no internal AIVMeta files are required. SHA-256 checksum. Windows 10/11 x64, an MP4 player, and Internet access are required.

Sharing flows — sending currently disabled

Inbox / Draft: authorize video upload, select your video and expressly consent to sending it to your TikTok Inbox. Open the notification inside TikTok to finish captioning, settings and posting. Sending a draft is not publication.

Direct Post: authorize this flow separately. Review your connected nickname and fresh creator information, edit the caption, choose privacy manually, and enable only allowed interactions. Comments, Duet and Stitch start unchecked. Complete applicable disclosures and consent before the final review and send action.

You control each transfer

Choose your own MP4 and preview it. No course IDs, content library, schedules or other social platform setup are required. Your video goes directly to TikTok after consent. AIVMeta's broker handles authorization and posting metadata, but does not receive video bytes. Processing may take a few minutes; the desktop checks actual status.

Connect and disconnect your own account

Sign in only on TikTok's authorization page. AIVMeta does not ask for your TikTok password. Use “Disconnect all my TikTok connections” to disable local posting and request deletion of broker credentials. If provider revocation is unavailable, also remove AIVMeta in TikTok's connected-app settings. Previously transferred content must be managed in TikTok.

Beta availability

Current support is Windows only. TikTok permissions, review status, account settings and platform limits can restrict available flows. No customer count, adoption or approval is claimed. This creator-facing tool is separate from AIVMeta's internal Publisher.
